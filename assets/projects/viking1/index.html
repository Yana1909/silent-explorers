August 20, 1975 • Cape Canaveral, Florida

Blast Off on Titan IIIE-Centaur!

Together with my twin Orbiter partner, I weighed about 3,500 kilograms when fully fueled. A Titan IIIE-Centaur rocket launched us from Cape Canaveral, Florida, and we headed for Mars.

💡 Fun Fact: I have a twin! Viking 2 landed on Mars on September 3, 1976, at Utopia Planitia.

Gadget #1 • Cameras

My Robot Eyes

Two 360-degree cylindrical scan cameras sat on my body. Twenty-five seconds after I landed, I began sending my first picture from the surface of Mars.

🌟 Kid-Friendly Comparison:

NASA calls it the first photograph ever taken from the surface of Mars, and my later pictures made the first panorama of the surface.
