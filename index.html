About this project

Every glowing point on this globe is a real machine NASA (or its partners) sent to the Moon or Mars — and never brought home. Drag to look around, then tap a marker to hear its story, see its last photo, and read what it really did.

Built for ages 10–14 Approximate real landing coordinates

About this project. Made for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — "Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars." Dates, mission durations, and outcomes are drawn from public NASA/JPL mission records. Photos are public-domain NASA imagery. First-person narration is a storytelling device; it dramatizes real events rather than inventing new ones.

One documented myth is corrected on purpose: Opportunity's famous "my battery is low and it's getting dark" line was never an actual transmission from the rover — see its card for what was really sent.
